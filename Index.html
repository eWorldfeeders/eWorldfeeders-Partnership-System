Agricultural Partnership Areas

The portal is for agricultural enquiries and proposals. Specific projects, resources, and terms are assessed and confirmed individually.

Discuss agricultural production, supply networks, infrastructure, irrigation, processing, logistics, market access, or institutional projects.

AgroTech Partnership Categories

Choose the category that best matches your agricultural proposal. Submitting an application expresses interest only; every proposal is subject to review.

Category I

Contract Planter

For applicants interested in approved agricultural production or contract-planting arrangements.

Project-specific assessment and terms
  • Assessment may consider crop, location, land availability, experience, capacity, and resources.
  • Responsibilities, budget, timeline, and terms must be discussed and documented.
  • No guaranteed farm access, harvest, produce purchase, or profit.
Agricultural Distribution
Category II

Regional Distributor

For individuals or businesses proposing to distribute approved agricultural products in an agreed area.

Terms confirmed in writing per product/project
  • Assessment may consider territory, customer access, market experience, storage, and delivery capacity.
  • Product availability, pricing, payment terms, margins, minimum orders, and exclusivity are not assumed.
  • A request for distribution rights is not an approved distributorship.
Category III

Enterprise Partner

For organizations, businesses, project sponsors, institutional partners, and parties proposing larger collaborations.

Proposal-based review
  • Possible discussion areas include production, infrastructure, irrigation, processing, logistics, and market access.
  • Each proposal and any proposed capital or resource contribution requires separate assessment.
  • No investment, ownership, revenue share, or returns arise from an application alone.

Commercial terms—including wholesale prices, distributor margins, territory, payment arrangements, minimum orders, responsibilities, and any incentives—must be confirmed for the relevant product or project before activation.

How the Partnership Process Works

These are general stages; the exact process may vary by project. No fixed approval timeline or acceptance is promised.

  1. 1. Select a category

    Choose Contract Planter, Regional Distributor, Enterprise Partner, or another supported category.

  2. 2. Submit your interest

    Provide accurate contact details and information relevant to your agricultural proposal.

  3. 3. Application review

    EWF reviews suitability, project availability, location, experience, resources, and alignment.

  4. 4. Verification and discussion

    Additional information may be requested and the proposed arrangement discussed.

  5. 5. Written terms and approval

    If suitable, the parties clarify responsibilities, costs, commercial terms, timelines, and conditions.

  6. 6. Activation

    Work begins only after required approvals, documentation, and other applicable requirements are complete.

Important Partnership Information

  • All applications are subject to review and approval. An application alone creates no partnership, agency, employment, distributorship, investment, or ownership relationship.
  • No applicant is automatically entitled to farm allocation, a distribution territory, commission, salary, profit share, or investment return.
  • Any payment, margin, fee, revenue share, or other compensation, and each party’s responsibilities, deliverables, timing, and risks, must be expressly agreed in writing by authorized representatives before work begins.
  • Availability and terms may vary by location, product, season, budget, and operational capacity. No agricultural outcome, yield, sales, profit, or return is guaranteed.
  • Provide accurate information and use EWF’s name, logo, or materials only with appropriate authorization.

Website content is general information. Specific rights and obligations are determined by the applicable signed agreement. Have final legal terms reviewed by a qualified legal professional familiar with the relevant jurisdiction.

Privacy and Contact
