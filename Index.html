Find the right partnership category

Select the category that best matches your proposal. Applying expresses interest only; every proposal is subject to review and written confirmation.

Category I

Contract Planter

For applicants interested in suitable agricultural production or contract-planting arrangements.

Project-specificAssessment, responsibilities and terms
  • Assessment may consider crop, location, land access, experience, capacity and resources.
  • Budgets, responsibilities, timelines and obligations must be discussed and documented.
  • No guaranteed farm access, harvest, produce purchase or profit.
Agricultural distribution
Category II

Regional Distributor

For individuals and businesses proposing to distribute approved agricultural products in an agreed area.

Written terms requiredProduct, territory and commercial conditions
  • Assessment may consider territory, customer access, market experience, storage and delivery capacity.
  • Pricing, margins, payment terms, minimum orders and exclusivity are not assumed.
  • An application does not grant distributorship or exclusive territory rights.
Category III

Enterprise Partner

For organizations, businesses, institutional partners and project sponsors proposing larger collaborations.

Proposal-basedAssessment and structured discussion
  • Potential areas include production, infrastructure, irrigation, processing, logistics and market access.
  • Proposals and any capital or resource contribution require separate assessment.
  • No investment, ownership, revenue share or return arises from an application alone.

Wholesale prices, distributor margins, territory, payment arrangements, minimum orders, responsibilities and any incentives must be confirmed in writing for the relevant product or project before activation.

From enquiry to agreement

A clear process, step by step

These are general stages. Requirements and timelines may vary by project, and acceptance is not guaranteed.

  1. Select a category

    Choose Contract Planter, Regional Distributor, Enterprise Partner or discuss a relevant proposal.

  2. Submit your enquiry

    Provide accurate contact details and information relevant to your agricultural proposal.

  3. Application review

    The proposal may be assessed for suitability, location, experience, resources and project availability.

  4. Verification & discussion

    Additional information may be requested and the proposed arrangement discussed.

  5. Agree written terms

    If suitable, clarify responsibilities, costs, commercial terms, timelines and conditions in writing.

  6. Activate the arrangement

    Work begins only after required approvals, documentation and applicable requirements are complete.

Please read

Important partnership information

  • All applications are subject to review and approval. An application alone creates no partnership, agency, employment, distributorship, investment or ownership relationship.
  • No applicant is automatically entitled to farm allocation, a distribution territory, commission, salary, profit share or investment return.
  • Payments, margins, fees, revenue shares, responsibilities, deliverables, timing and risks must be expressly agreed in writing by authorized representatives before work begins.
  • Availability and terms may vary by location, product, season, budget and operational capacity. No yield, sales, profit or return is guaranteed.
  • Provide accurate information and use EWF's name, logo or materials only with appropriate authorization.

Website content is general information. Specific rights and obligations are determined by the applicable written agreement. Have final legal terms reviewed by a qualified legal professional familiar with the relevant jurisdiction.
