Frequently Asked Questions

What to know before you enquire.

For accurate and current details about a particular training arrangement, contact EWF Innovation & Skills Academy directly.

What is EWF Innovation & Skills Academy?
EWF Innovation & Skills Academy is a technology and innovation skills-development initiative connected to the EWorldfeeders Global Link ecosystem. The website introduces training areas and provides a direct enquiry route for current course details.
What training areas are available?
The catalogue covers AI, software and web development, business systems, digital marketing, ICT infrastructure, AgroTech, renewable energy, Web3, and related subjects. Ask the Academy to confirm the current availability of your chosen area.
How can I obtain the curriculum, duration, and fees?
Use a course enquiry button or the official WhatsApp contact. The Academy can advise on the current curriculum, requirements, duration, delivery options, fees, and next steps.
Are training sessions online or in person?
Delivery options may depend on the particular course and arrangement. Please confirm the available format directly before registering.
Do I need previous experience?
Prerequisites can differ by training area. Contact the Academy with your chosen course and current experience so the applicable requirements can be clarified.
How do I register?
Begin by enquiring about your chosen course. After the details are confirmed, follow the registration instructions provided by the Academy.
Are certificates available?
Certificate availability and completion requirements depend on the specific training arrangement. Please confirm the applicable details before enrolling.
Can organizations request training?
Organizations and businesses can contact EWF Innovation & Skills Academy to enquire about training needs. Any customized programme, scope, schedule, and fee would need to be discussed and confirmed.
